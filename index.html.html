The Commerce360 story

From shelf to sale.
From purchase to performance.

The product is designed around the commercial cycle of an SME — not a collection of disconnected screens.

01 — SEE

Start with the whole business.

The executive dashboard brings revenue, open orders, inventory alerts, stock value, sales, purchases, payments and operational indicators into one management view.

02 — SELL

One catalog becomes the customer experience.

Product names, SKUs, categories, prices, stock status and active visibility are managed centrally. What your team controls becomes what your customer sees.

03 — STOCK

Know what you have before the customer asks.

Inventory brings quantity on hand, valuation, reorder thresholds and stock health together. Low-stock and out-of-stock states can drive both internal action and customer-facing status.

04 — BUY

Purchasing that feeds the operation.

Purchase invoices, returns, suppliers and batches live inside the same system. Procurement activity feeds supplier balances and inventory quantities instead of becoming another data island.

05 — FULFIL

Turn customer demand into controlled fulfilment.

Storefront orders arrive in Orders Management for confirmation, invoicing, returns and customer handling. Public order tracking reflects the operational status maintained by the team.

06 — UNDERSTAND

See performance, not just transactions.

Flexible reporting surfaces gross sales, returns, net sales, transaction volume and trends — converting day-to-day activity into commercial visibility.
